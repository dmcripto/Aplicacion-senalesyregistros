VELTRIX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

VELTRIX is a personal trading journal that lets you log your trades by hand or receive them automatically from a TradingView alert. This page explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

What data we collect

  • Email and password — to create your account and sign in (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Your trade data — symbol, direction, entry price, take profit, stop loss, result and notes that you enter or that arrive through your personal TradingView webhook.
  • Money settings (optional) — starting capital, risk per trade and currency, if you choose to enter them to see results in money.
  • Exchange API keys (optional) — if you connect Binance, Bybit, Bitunix, MEXC, Gate, Bitget, OKX or KuCoin: your read-only API key and secret (and the API password, if the exchange asks for one), stored encrypted on the server, and the closed trades imported with them. VELTRIX rejects keys that allow trading or withdrawing where the exchange lets us check it (otherwise we ask you to confirm it) and never trades on your behalf.
  • Time zone — your device's, only to send you the daily summary at 9 PM your time. You can turn the summary off in Settings.
  • AI coach (optional) — when you ask for an analysis, statistics calculated from your closed trades (for example, results by day and time) are sent to an AI service. Your exchange keys, email and other personal data are not sent. The analysis text is stored in your account.
  • WhatsApp (optional) — if you turn on signals on WhatsApp: your phone number, which is confirmed with a code we send you on WhatsApp, and whether alerts are on or paused. Alerts carry the asset, levels and result in R of your signals, never your money. You can pause them by replying BAJA or disconnect the number at any time.
  • Telegram community (optional) — if you connect a group or channel, the bot posts there your account's signals (asset, entry, TP and SL) and their results in R. It does not post your money or personal data, and you can disconnect it at any time.
  • Telegram (optional) — if you connect the VELTRIX bot: your chat identifier and Telegram username, to send you alerts and log the signals you send it. You can disconnect it at any time.
  • Error diagnostics — if the app or the web fails unexpectedly, the technical error message, version and platform are stored against your account, only to fix the problem.
  • Preferences — your language, daily limits and auto-close setting.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier (Expo Push Token) so we can notify you when an alert arrives.

We do not collect location data or contacts, and we do not access your camera or gallery.

How we use this data

Only to provide the app's core function: saving your trade journal, calculating your statistics and sending you a notification when a new trade is logged. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not analyze it for commercial purposes.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure, encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your own trades is restricted by database-level security rules (Row Level Security): no other user can see your data.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only external services that process data on your behalf are Supabase (database and authentication), Expo (sending push notifications), Anthropic (only if you use the AI coach: it receives statistics calculated from your journal, never your keys or email) and, if you connect it, Telegram (alerts and bot) and WhatsApp, by Meta (confirmation code and alerts to your number) — all as infrastructure providers, not as final recipients of your information.

To show how close the price is to your TP or SL, your browser directly asks Binance for the public price of the cryptocurrencies open in your journal (only the asset name; Binance can see your IP address, as with any visit). No account data is sent.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all your data at any time from the app or the web (how to do it), or ask us for a copy of your data by writing to the contact below.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will reflect the changes on this page with a new update date.
