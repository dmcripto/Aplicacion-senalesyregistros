VELTRIX — Delete account and data

How to delete your account and all your information

From the app or the web (recommended)

  1. Sign in to VELTRIX.
  2. In the app: Settings tab → Delete my account and data. On the web: at the bottom of the page, Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password).
  • All your trades and journal notes.
  • Your money settings, preferences and daily limits.
  • Any exchange API keys you connected.
  • Your TradingView webhook URL.
  • The identifiers of your devices for push notifications.

We do not keep copies of this data once the account is deleted.

If you cannot sign in

Write to dmcripto925@gmail.com from the email you registered with, asking for deletion, and we will delete it within 30 days at most.
